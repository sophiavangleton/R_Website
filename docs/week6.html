About this data

This data comes from a real experiment from our lab, it is data from a task-switching experiment collected last year. It contains 14 columns corresponding to different variables that are important to keep track of:

  • id: subject ID

  • block: blocks in the experiment

  • trial: trials in the experiment

  • var: ok so this one actually doesnt correspond to anything lol

  • practice: practice blocks, there are two practice blocks at the beginning of each experiment

  • csi: cue stimulus interval. This refers to the cue instruction for the task-switching design either being presented at stimulus onset (short) or before stimulus onset (long)

  • task: refers to which task was being presented (size or parity were the two tasks)

  • numpresent: what stimulus was present. Could be c(1,2,3,4,6,7,8,9) but not 5 because the task was to either determine if the stimulus presented was larger or smaller than 5 (size task) OR if the number present was odd or even (parity)

  • cor_res: what would have been the correct response

  • rt: response time

  • act_res: what the actual response was by the participant

  • error: if an error occurred (0 = no error, 1 = error)

  • act_res_again: this one doesn’t matter, its just the actual response again lol

  • points: to account for attentional interest throughout the experiment, we ‘gameified’ correct responses by adding in points, where they get 1 point every 3 correct responses
